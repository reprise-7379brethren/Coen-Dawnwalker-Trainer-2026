## 🎮 About the Game

The Blood of Dawnwalker is a single-player open-world dark fantasy action RPG developed by Rebel Wolves and published by Bandai Namco Entertainment. Set in fourteenth-century Europe, it follows Coen, a young man who becomes human by day and a vampire by night. The game launches on September 3, 2026, for PC, PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X|S.

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **The Blood of Dawnwalker**. This unofficial PC trainer provides optional assistance with Coen's health, stamina, activation charges, skill progression, vampiric hunger, corruption, and the campaign's limited time resource. It is designed for the single-player experience and lets players explore Vale Sangora, experiment with human and vampire abilities, and revisit difficult encounters with fewer progression restrictions.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to explore Vale Sangora freely, test human and vampire builds, reduce difficulty during combat, experiment with the narrative sandbox, or replay quests without strict progression pressure.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Coen can travel through villages, forests, swamps, mountains, caves, crypts, ruins, and the city of Svartrau. Human and vampire forms provide different traversal options, with nighttime abilities enabling movement across rooftops and access to elevated areas. Shadowstep, Clawride, and Planeshift expand nighttime mobility. Fast travel is available through the world map, and the game does not use a horse.

### Tools & Inventory

Loot equipment, resources, manuals, magical rings, and amulets during exploration and encounters. Swordmastery manuals improve Coen's sword abilities, while magic manuals develop witchcraft. Rings and amulets can enhance attributes and have different rarity levels. Use inventory-related trainer options carefully when modifying progression or collected items, and save before making changes.

### World & Environment

Vale Sangora is an open narrative sandbox divided into regions controlled by powerful vampires. The complete map is visible from the beginning, but activities and points of interest must be discovered through exploration, clues, local information, and investigations. The campaign provides thirty in-game days and thirty nights to save Coen's family. Time advances at specific quest and objective checkpoints rather than continuously during free exploration.

### Combat & Weapons

Combat combines directional swordplay, blocking, parrying, stamina management, active abilities, witchcraft, and vampiric powers. Successful attacks, blocks, and parries can generate Activation Charges for abilities such as Burning Blood and Artery Attack. Human combat emphasizes swordsmanship and witchcraft, while nighttime combat uses claws and vampiric abilities. Coen can also fight with his fists, and the game rewards precise timing, positioning, and awareness of attack direction.
